Blue Horizon

An adventure beyond the porthole.

The project

A steampunk submarine adventure conceived as an interactive escape-room experience aboard a cruise ship. A crew of four to eight guests journeys through a fantasy world of treasure, puzzles, and a kraken encounter, with the experience developed through story, guest flow, spatial planning, and show elements.

Design & development

Story meets spatial design

The floorplan organizes the experience into parallel paths through engine rooms, navigation decks, captain’s quarters, and a finale. Selected presentation pages show the connection between narrative beats and the guest journey.

Selected presentation pages are shown in their original context. Collaborative work and reference imagery are identified in the project credits and captions.
