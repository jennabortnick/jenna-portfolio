Traveling Escape Room

Taking the experience on the road.

The project

A collaborative feasibility study for a traveling escape-room experience operating in major cities. The project considers what it takes to move a themed attraction from an idea into a workable business, connecting the visitor experience with operations, logistics, and financial assumptions.

Behind the experience

The study examines city selection, space and capacity, staffing, transportation, intellectual-property considerations, capital costs, and sensitivity testing. The selected slides document the team’s planning approach.
