Yellow Submarine

Bar & Lounge

An escape beneath the surface.

The project

An immersive, psychedelic cocktail-bar concept inspired by The Beatles’ Yellow Submarine. The experience invites adult guests to leave the everyday behind and enter an underwater world shaped by music, color, and a sequence of imaginative spaces.
