Design & development

From mini golf to an immersive adventure

The concept begins with a question: how can mini golf become more than nine separate holes? In Outlaw City, players are invited to try out for Pecos Bill’s outlaw gang. Each hole becomes a trial on the path to proving yourself as an outlaw, connecting the gameplay to a larger story and themed world.

Site analysis & spatial planning

Designing for the SCI Lawn

The course is being developed for the SCI Lawn, a designated site with existing conditions that shape the design. Slopes, trees, and perimeter lights all influence the available footprint and the placement of the nine holes. Our team is using a site plan, measured dimensions, and a 3D scan of the lawn to understand the space and make informed layout decisions. My focus is bringing the course together as a cohesive plan while considering circulation, gameplay, and buildability.

Digital development

Developing holes, gameplay & models

We are brainstorming individual hole concepts, sketching layouts, developing gameplay mechanics, and building digital models. I use Blender to work on both the placement of the holes across the site and the individual hole models. Our team also uses Rhino, sharing software-specific responsibilities according to each person's experience. As the designs develop, we are evaluating how the digital models can translate into physical construction.

Collaboration & next steps

Coordination, communication & the build ahead

Alongside my design work, I lead meetings, track progress, and coordinate between the art and gameplay teams to keep communication clear and everyone informed. The project has reinforced how much time creative and construction tasks can take, and how important realistic planning and consistent communication are to keeping a collaborative project moving. The course is still in development; the next phase will bring the designs into hands-on construction, where I plan to contribute primarily to the build and, hopefully, additional coordination.

The course is in development; images and design documentation will be added as the project progresses. The portfolio will continue to grow as new work is documented.
