As the Trees Grow

A Fairy Tale Parade

Seven stories. One enchanted forest.

The project

A collaborative parade concept inspired by Grimm’s fairy tales. Seven story-inspired floats bring together performance, music, costumes, and puppetry around a shared theme: venturing into the unknown to gain self-determination.

Design & development

Enter the forest

The concept moves through The Pied Piper, Rumpelstiltskin, Little Red Riding Hood, Rapunzel, the Town Musicians of Bremen, Hansel and Gretel, and The Twelve Dancing Princesses. Scenic and performance references help establish a common woodland world.
